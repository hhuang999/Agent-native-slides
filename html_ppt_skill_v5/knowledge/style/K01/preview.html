Market Premium Analysis

Properties Within 200 Meters of Water — River, Lake, or Harbor — Command a 34 Percent Premium Over Comparable Inland Units and Have Demonstrated Negative Correlation With General Real Estate Market Downturns Across Every Post-2000 Correction.

7 Corrections Analysed

Across all seven market corrections occurring between 2001 and 2023 — including the global financial crisis, the COVID-19 liquidity contraction, and three regional sovereign-debt episodes — waterfront-proximate assets in the Fund's covered markets either appreciated or declined by a materially smaller margin than non-waterfront comparables. The hedging value of water proximity is most pronounced in Geneva lakefront and Singapore Marina Basin assets.

Supplementary Finding
£2.9M Avg. Absolute Premium Per Asset

In absolute terms, the waterfront premium averaged £2.9 million per London asset and CHF 3.1 million per Geneva asset over the study period. Premium compression was not observed even during peak rate-tightening cycles, suggesting structural inelasticity in buyer demand at this price tier.

Sample: 214 waterfront-proximate and 633 inland comparable transactions. Adjustments applied for floor, age, and amenity tier.
