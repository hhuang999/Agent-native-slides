Nordic Wellness & Hospitality Group — Guest Experience Study

Guests Who Stayed Five or More Nights at Nordic Wellness Properties With Integrated Thermal Bathing Facilities Reported 74 Percent Higher Brand Loyalty Scores and Generated 2.9 Times the Annual Revenue per Guest of Single-Stay Visitors.

A multi-property longitudinal study tracked 4,800 guests across six Nordic Wellness Group properties from 2021 to 2024. Retention, revenue, and satisfaction data were collected at 30, 90, and 365 days post-stay, stratified by stay duration and thermal facility engagement.

Regression analysis across all six properties confirms a statistically significant dose-response relationship between thermal session frequency and guest satisfaction metrics. Properties reporting the highest average session counts per stay also recorded the steepest year-over-year growth in advance bookings, reducing their dependence on last-minute discount campaigns and improving forward revenue visibility by an average of 23 percentage points.

Guests who completed three or more thermal sessions per stay were 4.1 times more likely to purchase a multi-visit membership package than those with a single session.
