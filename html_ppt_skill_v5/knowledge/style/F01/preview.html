Chain-of-thought length predicts accuracy ceiling

Across 47 benchmarks, models that generate longer intermediate reasoning chains consistently reach higher accuracy ceilings — independent of parameter count.

Accuracy lift (CoT vs direct) +31% averaged over math benchmarks
Reasoning tokens / answer token 8.4× for top-quartile performers
Benchmarks evaluated 47 across math, code, science, logic
