02 / 04 — PERFORMANCE METRICS

At 68% repeat guest rate — more than double the US independent hotel average of 31% — Vellichor's acquisition cost per guest has declined every year since 2021.

Vellichor's model inverts the typical hospitality cost structure: instead of allocating 18–22% of revenue to OTA commissions and brand fees, the group spends that budget on staff tenure, building restoration depth, and a proprietary direct-booking platform that drove 84% direct revenue in 2025. The result is a guest relationship that no chain flag can replicate — guests book Vellichor properties the way they return to a restaurant run by a chef they trust, not because of a loyalty points program.
