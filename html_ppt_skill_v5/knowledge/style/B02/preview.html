02 / 04 — Clinical Results

In a 90-day randomized trial, 87% of participants reported measurable improvement in skin barrier function — with no irritation events.

Our circadian formulation model splits the product line into AM and PM phases, each timed to the skin's natural enzymatic cycle. Morning formulas support antioxidant defense during UV exposure; evening formulas concentrate repair and barrier ceramides when transepidermal water loss peaks. The clinical trial (n=210, 90 days, double-blind, Fitzpatrick I–VI) showed no skin tone bias in efficacy outcomes — a first in independent barrier studies at this price point.
