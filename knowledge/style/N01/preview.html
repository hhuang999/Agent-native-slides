The correlation between museum institutional recognition and long-run price appreciation reflects the market's appetite for curatorial validation as a proxy for lasting cultural significance. Our analysis tracked 847 debut artists across the IAH Global Registry from 2010–2023, controlling for medium, geographic origin, and initial auction estimate range. Artists whose debut was followed by a solo retrospective at a Tier-1 institution — defined as a museum with an acquisition budget exceeding $10 million annually — demonstrated compound annual appreciation of 18.7 percent, compared with 5.5 percent for the control cohort. The differential persisted across all measured economic cycles, including the 2020 market contraction.
