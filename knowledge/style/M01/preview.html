Key Findings

Each Additional Gallery Conversation Session Per Visitor Increases Long-Term Membership Conversion Rate by 12 Percentage Points and Reduces Lapsed Membership Rate by 18 Percent Over the Following 24 Months.

Average incremental annual revenue per visitor who attended three or more facilitated conversations, compared to one-session participants — driven by renewals, donations, and event bookings.

Membership data matched to visit logs across a 24-month follow-up window show a dose-response relationship between facilitated session attendance and long-term retention. Visitors completing three or more sessions achieved membership renewal rates of 78 percent compared to 44 percent in the control cohort. The financial model indicates full program cost recovery at approximately 1.6 sessions per converted member.
