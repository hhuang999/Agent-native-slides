02 / 04 — Quantitative Findings

Three-hop beneficial ownership is the decisive structural marker: every seizure in the dataset was preceded by at least one intermediary registered in a non-disclosure jurisdiction within 18 months of the primary transaction.

Across all 14,200 transactions, 11,330 (79.8%) involved at least one beneficial owner obscured behind a nominee director. Of those, 94% were ultimately associated with confirmed seizure orders. The remaining 6% involved partial asset recovery where primary account holders cooperated with jurisdiction authorities before full trace completion. The 18-month registration window appears across all 39 jurisdictions regardless of local corporate disclosure requirements — suggesting a coordinated operational pattern rather than independent opportunism.

Non-disclosure jurisdictions
Top-5 intermediary registrations: Seychelles, Belize, Marshall Islands, St. Kitts & Nevis, Vanuatu. All below FATF threshold for nominee director disclosure.
SAR correlation rate
61% of flagged transactions had a corresponding FinCEN SAR filed within 90 days. SAR-correlated cases resolved 2.3× faster in asset recovery proceedings.
Shelf corporation age
78% of nominee-director intermediaries were registered between 12 and 24 months prior to the flagged transaction. Older shelves (5+ years) present in only 4% of cases.
Wire amount threshold
Median flagged transaction: $218,000. 91% fall between $50,000 and $990,000 — consistently below CTR reporting thresholds in all jurisdictions.
