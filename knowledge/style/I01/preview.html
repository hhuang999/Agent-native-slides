Architectural Heritage Foundation Conservation Economics Report 2024

Adaptive Reuse Projects Deliver 28 Percent Higher Net Present Value per Square Meter Than Comparable New Construction in Central Urban Locations, While Generating 40 Percent Fewer Embodied Carbon Emissions.

This report synthesises findings from 22 evaluated adaptive reuse programs across seven metropolitan areas. Analysis demonstrates compelling financial and environmental advantages for heritage building conversion over equivalent new-build schemes when controlling for location, typology, and programme mix.

Higher Occupancy Rate in Converted Heritage Stock

Projects situated within 400 metres of a rail or rapid-transit station demonstrated the strongest combined performance: shorter payback periods, lower vacancy risk, and sustained lease premiums averaging 12–17 percent above comparable new-build floor space. The heritage typology conferred tenant retention advantages tied to architectural character and reduced construction disruption costs relative to greenfield development.
