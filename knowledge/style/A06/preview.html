03 / 07 — KEY FINDINGS

The upper 2000m warmed by 15 ZJ in 2024 — roughly 40 times humanity's annual energy consumption.

Argo profiling floats sampled 3.9 million temperature–salinity profiles in 2024, providing full-depth ocean coverage for the first time since the program's inception. Upper-ocean heat content anomalies show consistent poleward migration of the 20°C isotherm, with tropical Pacific deep mixing events increasingly rare as stratification strengthens.
