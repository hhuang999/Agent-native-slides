02 Evidence

Each Cohort of Trained Teachers Improves Student Completion Rates by 6.4 Percentage Points and Retains Effects Across at Least Three Subsequent School Years.

Regression analysis across 312 districts shows that each 10-percentage-point increase in the share of teachers who completed the structured in-service training program is associated with a 6.4pp rise in Grade 6 completion rates in the following academic year. The effect persists across a minimum of three cohorts without additional intervention, suggesting durable pedagogical change rather than a short-term Hawthorne effect.

Gender Gap Closure
−11pp

The gender completion gap narrowed from 19 points (2010) to 8 points (2023). Districts with the highest community school construction density show the steepest narrowing — median 14pp closure vs. 7pp in low-construction districts.
