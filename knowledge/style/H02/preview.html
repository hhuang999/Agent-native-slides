PPP Evaluation Report — Series 14 · Broadband Infrastructure

Public-Private Partnerships in Broadband Infrastructure Delivered Connection Costs 34 Percent Below Fully Public Procurement While Achieving Deployment Timelines 28 Months Faster Across All 14 Regional Programs.

This report presents findings from the Authority's independent evaluation of 14 regional broadband PPP programs completed between 2019 and 2024, covering 3.2 million households across urban, peri-urban, and rural coverage zones. Cost and timeline performance is benchmarked against equivalent fully public procurement initiatives conducted over the same period.

Finding 02 — Contract Structure

PPP Broadband Programs That Used Output-Based Aid Mechanisms Reached 94 Percent of Target Rural Households Within Budget, Compared to 71 Percent for Input-Based Contract Structures.

94%
Rural household coverage under output-based aid contracts
71%
Rural household coverage under input-based contract structures
+23pp
Coverage gap attributable to contract structure alone

Output-based aid mechanisms align private operator incentives directly with coverage outcomes by disbursing public subsidy only upon verified connection milestones. Evaluation data across eight OBA-structured programs show consistent over-performance on household coverage targets, with budget adherence averaging 97 percent. Input-based contracts, by contrast, disburse on procurement milestones rather than deployment outcomes, reducing operators' financial exposure to coverage shortfalls and producing a statistically significant divergence in final rural reach.

Budget Adherence — OBA Programs
97%

Across all eight output-based aid programs, final expenditure remained within 3 percent of approved budget envelopes — compared to a 14 percent average overrun recorded in input-based equivalents evaluated over the same period.
