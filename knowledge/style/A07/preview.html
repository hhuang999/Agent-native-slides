02 / 05 — MATERIAL PROPERTIES

Three independent properties engineered into a single filament — no coatings, no laminate.

Property 01
Diffractive Iridescence
120°
The filament diffracts visible light across a 120° hue range as viewing angle changes. No dye required — color is structural, fade-resistant, and wash-stable to 80°C.
Structure-based coloration
Property 02
Thermal Chromism
±8°C
An embedded thermochromic polymer shifts the dominant wavelength by 35nm for every 8°C change in surface temperature — the fabric reads the body's heat map visibly.
Leuco-dye free; biopolymer
Property 03
Tensile Strength
4.2 GPa
The diffractive microstructure is co-extruded with a high-tenacity polyamide core, yielding tensile strength comparable to carbon fiber at one-fifth the density.
Aerospace-grade core
