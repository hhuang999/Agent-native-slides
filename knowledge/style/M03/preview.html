Independent Design Festival Foundation  •  Economic Impact Study 2021–2024

Independent Design Festivals in Tier-2 Cities Generated 4.1 Times Their Direct Funding Investment in Measurable Local Economic Activity Between 2021 and 2024, With the Greatest Multiplier Effect in Cities With Established Creative Education Infrastructure.

This study examined 34 independent design festivals across 19 tier-2 cities over a four-year period, measuring direct and indirect economic outputs against total public and private funding inputs. The findings establish a robust causal link between festival investment, local creative infrastructure, and sustained economic activity beyond the event window.

Visitor expenditure tracking was conducted via anonymised card-transaction data from host-city merchant networks, supplemented by post-festival survey panels (n = 4,820). Hospitality spending — accommodation, food and beverage, and local transport — accounted for 68% of the total; independent retail and maker-market sales contributed the remaining 32%. The relocation-consideration metric correlates most strongly with festival programming that includes open-studio and live-practice elements rather than pure exhibition formats.
