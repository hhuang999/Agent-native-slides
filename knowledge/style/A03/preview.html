ICLR 2025 · Oral Presentation · Track: Efficient ML

Sparse Transformers Scale Reliably
to One Trillion Parameters

We present a mixture-of-experts architecture that achieves dense-model accuracy while activating only 3% of parameters per token, validated across language, vision, and multimodal tasks at scales from 7B to 1T.

Priya Nair, James Okafor, Lin Zhao Stanford HAI · Google DeepMind arxiv:2502.00417
03 / 09 — MAIN RESULT

Activating 3% of parameters achieves 99.1% of dense-model accuracy at 7× lower compute cost.

On a suite of 12 language benchmarks, our 1T-parameter sparse model matches a 140B dense model in average accuracy while consuming 7.2× fewer FLOPs per token. The routing mechanism generalizes to vision encoders and audio models without architectural changes.
