Three operating principles

Restraint produces clarity

Each decision to remove is worth more than ten decisions to add. A page that communicates one thing clearly beats one that communicates five things poorly.

02 Whitespace is structure, not emptiness. Generous margins signal intent.
03 Color carries meaning. One accent hue, used sparingly, creates direction.
