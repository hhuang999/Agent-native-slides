02 / 04 — Studio Profile

Phantasm has produced 47 campaigns that outlasted the trends they referenced — average client relationship: 6.4 years.

The studio's model is the inverse of trend-chasing: we mine a client's cultural moment for its persistent emotional logic, then translate that into a visual system anchored in timeless geometric forms and a palette that ages deliberately. The 80s and 90s aesthetics we work with are not costume — they are tested emotional registers with documented recall durability.
